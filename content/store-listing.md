---

Mahabharat Katha & Gita brings India's greatest epic to your phone in simple Hindi and English, fully offline.

• Complete Bhagavad Gita: all 18 chapters with summaries, key Sanskrit shlokas and meanings
• 18 parvas of the Mahabharat retold as 60+ short stories, each with a life lesson
• 45 character profiles with relationships and story links
• Daily shloka and lesson
• Read aloud, bookmarks, share, dark mode, adjustable text size
• No login, no internet, no data collection

Sanskrit text follows the traditional accepted edition. Stories are original retellings based on the Vyasa Mahabharata.
